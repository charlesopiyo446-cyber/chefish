What We Offer

From fresh supply to delivery and bulk contracts we cover everything you need.

Fresh Delivery

Fast delivery of fresh tilapia to your home, business, hotel, restaurant, or event venue. Place your order on WhatsApp and we will confirm delivery timing and location.

Cleaning & Gutting

We clean, scale, and gut your fish on request so it arrives ready to season and cook. No mess, no hassle. Just let us know how you want it prepared when you order.

📦

Bulk & Wholesale Orders

Special pricing for restaurants, hotels, schools, and event caterers. We can fulfil large orders quickly and offer regular scheduled supply arrangements at agreed prices.

Fresh & Frozen Options

Our tilapia is available fresh daily. Frozen stock is also available on request for customers who need to plan ahead or store fish for later use.

WhatsApp & Phone Ordering

Regular Supply Contracts

Need tilapia every week or every day? We offer regular supply contracts for businesses that want a reliable, consistent fish supply at a fixed agreed price.
