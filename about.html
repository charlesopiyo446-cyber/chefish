About Us

Our story, mission, and the values we build our business on.

Our Story

Cheng Kelo Fresh Fish is a trusted local fish supplier based in Anaka Town Council, Nwoya District. We are located opposite Kiza Enterprise, on the eastern side of Market Mall House, Room No. 18 easy to find and always open.

We started with a simple goal: give the people of Anaka and surrounding communities access to the freshest tilapia at honest, fair prices. Every fish we sell is sourced fresh, handled carefully, and prepared to your preference whole, cleaned, gutted, or scaled.

Over the years we have grown into a reliable supplier for families, hotels, restaurants, schools, and event caterers. Whether you need a single fish or a bulk order for a wedding, we are ready to serve you.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, affordable, high-quality tilapia to every household and business in Anaka and beyond with fast service, clean handling, and a smile.

Why Tilapia?

Tilapia is nutritious, affordable, and loved across Uganda. We specialise in tilapia because we know it well — from the best sizes and weights to how to prepare it exactly the way our customers want it.
